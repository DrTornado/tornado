Tornado — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Tornado does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Tornado — your word list, review progress, audio notes, and generated audio — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. None of it is sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on sync.

Optional sync with your own GitHub repository

If you choose to enter a GitHub access token in Settings, Tornado will read and write your word list and notes to a repository that you own and control. We operate no server and receive no copy of your data. The token is stored encrypted on your device using Android's hardware-backed keystore and is sent only to api.github.com.

You can remove the token at any time from Settings. Sync is off until you add one.

Network requests the app makes

When you add a word or play audio, Tornado requests dictionary data from these public services. Each request contains only the word or sentence being looked up — never an identifier for you or your device:

  • freedictionaryapi.com — definitions, examples and pronunciations (Wiktionary data)
  • en.wiktionary.org — additional definitions
  • api.datamuse.com — synonyms, antonyms and spelling suggestions
  • tatoeba.org — example sentences
  • api.mymemory.translated.net — Arabic translations
  • commons.wikimedia.org — human pronunciation recordings
  • github.com — the optional premium voice download, and your own sync repository

These services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Internet & network state — to fetch dictionary data and sync
  • Foreground service & wake lock — to keep audio playing when the screen is off
  • Notifications — to show the media player controls while audio plays

Tornado requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

Tornado is a vocabulary learning tool suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
